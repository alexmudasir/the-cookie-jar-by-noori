# The Cookie Jar by Noori — Free Preview Website

A free preview website built for The Cookie Jar by Noori, a home bakery in Karachi.

## What it does

- Real menu: 10 cookies (Rs. 300–400), 3 tea cakes (Rs. 500–800) and 5 toppings
- Hero flavour switcher, menu, cart and Shopify-style checkout (order confirmation, no payment collected)
- Topping suggestions at checkout
- Real photos of the bakery's cookies

## Live demo

https://cookiejarbynori.netlify.app/

## Notes

- Built as a free preview for the bakery. The bakery can share its details and photos to get the complete website free.
- This is a demo; no payment is collected at checkout.
